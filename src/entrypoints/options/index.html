AI second opinion (optional)

A language model can read each day's titles and abstracts and give a second opinion, which the local model learns how far to trust. Use any provider, a model running on your own computer, or none at all: without one, everything runs locally and for free.

The two are linked on every listing: the summary bar shows which threshold expands the top N% of that day's papers, and what share of papers a threshold expands.

In Firefox: open Add-ons and themes, click the gear icon, then Manage Extension Shortcuts.

Authors

Papers by authors you follow always open expanded and get a ★; papers by authors you block are hidden. You can also follow or block from the menu that opens when you hover over an author's name on a listing. Names match by surname and initials: add other spellings as aliases, and link an INSPIRE profile to tell namesakes apart.
